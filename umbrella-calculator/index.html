How umbrella pay works

Where your money actually goes

Understanding the deductions helps you spot bad deals before you sign.

1. Your agency is invoiced

You submit your timesheet. Your umbrella invoices the agency at your contract rate for every hour or day worked.

2. Employer costs come out first

Employer National Insurance (15% above £5,000) and the apprenticeship levy are paid out of your assignment value before tax.

3. You're paid via PAYE

The rest is your gross salary. Income tax and employee NI are deducted and your net pay lands in your bank account.
