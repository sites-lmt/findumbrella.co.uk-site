Cookie policy

What cookies are, which ones this website uses, and how to control them. Last updated: September 2026.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function and let us understand how visitors use our pages.

Cookies we use

  • Essential cookies: required for the website to work. These cannot be switched off.
  • Third-party cookies: our quote form includes Cloudflare Turnstile, a bot-protection check that may set cookies or use similar technologies to tell people apart from automated traffic. We do not use third-party form providers.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies, which is why no cookie banner is shown.

Managing cookies

Because we only set essential and bot-protection cookies, there is nothing to opt out of on this site. You can still block or clear cookies in your browser at any time. Note that disabling cookies may affect how parts of the site work.
