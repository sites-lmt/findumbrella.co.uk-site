How umbrella payroll works

Effortless timesheets. We handle the rest.

Once you're matched, your umbrella company employs you for the life of your contract. You keep the freedom of contracting — finding your own roles and choosing when to work — while everything financial is handled for you.

Submit one timesheet a week. Your provider invoices the agency, runs payroll, files your RTI with HMRC and pays you. No accountant, no company admin, no tax return surprises.
