Assumptions & notes
  • 2026/27 rates: personal allowance £12,570, basic rate 20% to £50,270, employee NI 8%/2%, employer NI 15% above £5,000.
  • Inside IR35: PAYE umbrella with 12.07% holiday pay accrual and your chosen margin.
  • Outside IR35: company pays your chosen salary, all remaining profit distributed as dividends (£500 allowance, 10.75%/35.75%/39.35%).
  • Employment Allowance offsets employer NI on the limited company payroll (single director).
  • Accountancy and running costs are deducted from the outside-IR35 figure only.
